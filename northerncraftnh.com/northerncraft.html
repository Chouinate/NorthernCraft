About

Made in New Hampshire

Northern Craft started with a 3D printer and no real plan, just experimenting, making small things around the house, and learning as I went. It didn't take long to realize how much I enjoyed the design process, and I started looking for any excuse to create something new.

When my girlfriend wanted something to fill an empty wall at home, I jumped at the chance. I designed a set of geometric pieces, she loved them, and soon friends were asking where they could get them. That's when it started feeling like something worth sharing.

Every design is created digitally and printed to order in New Hampshire. Nothing gets released until it's been tested, adjusted, and approved by someone who won't spare my feelings.

If I wouldn't put it on my own wall, it doesn't make it into the collection.

How It Works

The Process

01

First Steps

Most pieces begin as a conversation with my girlfriend. We brainstorm the shapes together, usually trying to mix floral patterns with sharper, geometric lines. Once we have a direction, I start the digital work to turn that sketch into the final design.

02

Home Testing

A design has to work in a real room. I test every model in our own home to see how it actually looks on a wall in different lighting. If it doesn't look great on our wall, it doesn't get added to the collection.

03

Made for You

I don't keep a warehouse of stock. Every piece is created specifically for you once you order it. This allows me to focus on the details of a single work at a time rather than mass-producing a collection.

04

Hand Packed

I personally inspect and pack every order here in New Hampshire. Whether it's for your own home or a gift, I make sure it's handled with enough care to arrive ready to hang the moment it's opened.
