Shipping & Returns

Each piece is made by hand in my spare time — I genuinely appreciate your patience. I usually get things out sooner, but please allow up to 10–14 days for your order to ship.

United States

Flat $10 shipping on orders of 1–3 prints. Order four or more and shipping is on me.

Canada & International

Ships via USPS International. Rates are calculated at checkout based on your location and order size. Please note — import duties or taxes may be collected on delivery by your local carrier. Those fees are set by your country and aren't part of your order total.

Questions about your order? nate@northerncraftnh.com

Changed your mind? It happens. If the piece is in original condition, send it back within 14 days of delivery and I'll refund you. Return shipping is at your expense — I'm not able to provide return labels, especially for international orders.

Arrived damaged? Shoot me a photo and I'll take care of it. Replacement or refund, whatever works best for you.

Custom pieces are final sale, but if something's not right, reach out. I'll always work with you.

Questions? nate@northerncraftnh.com
